Thank you for testing

The training teaches Dynamics 365 Contact Center without a Dynamics 365 environment and without real phone numbers. Everything you set up, call and answer happens in a simulation in your browser. This page tells you how to get started, what to try and how to tell us what you found.

Getting started

  • Use a computer with a recent Edge, Chrome, Firefox or Safari. A large screen works best. Testing on Firefox or Safari is especially welcome, because we've tried those the least.
  • Plan about an hour for a first session. You can stop at any time and carry on later.
  • No account needed. You don't sign in. The Sign in button at the top is for trainers only.
  1. Open the training. A welcome screen asks how you want to work today.
  2. Choose Join a class if you got a class code, or Work on my own if not (see the next section).
  3. Not sure where things are? Choose First, show me around the screen on the welcome screen, or Tour at the top at any time. The tour shows each part of the screen; use Next, or Skip to stop.

In a class or on your own

Where your work is kept depends on this choice.

In a class

You have a class code (six letters and digits) from the person who runs the test.

  1. Choose Join a class.
  2. The first time: choose No, this is my first time, enter the class code and your first name, and select Join.
  3. You get a personal code. Write it down or copy it: you need it to carry on in another browser or on another computer.

✓ Your work on the class's labs and exercises is saved with the class.

The person running the test sees your progress and your answers.

Another browser or computer later? Choose Join a class › Yes, I joined before, and enter the class code and your personal code. Don't join again as new: you'd start over as a second person.

On your own

No code needed. Choose Work on my own and follow the labs in order, or pick practice exercises.

! Your work is kept in this browser only.

  • Coming back in the same browser: choose Continue on my own and you carry on where you left off.
  • To keep a copy, or to move to another computer: Files › Save to a file, and later Files › Open a saved file.
  • Clearing your browser's data, or using a private window, loses this work.

What to try

Open Labs and practice at the top. There are 11 labs, in a recommended order, from How a call finds its way to Putting it together. Each lab has three parts:

PartWhat you doWhat to look out for
LearnA few short pages with quotes from Microsoft Learn, and a quiz.Is it clear? Is anything wrong, missing or too long?
DoTasks in the simulated admin center, step by step, with checks that tick off.Do the checks tick off when you did it right? Are the steps clear?
ChallengeA task without step-by-step help.Too easy, too hard, or unclear what's asked?

A good first route

  1. Lab 1, all three parts.
  2. Labs 2 to 4, to set up a phone number, a workstream and a queue.
  3. Make a call on the Phone tab and answer it as a representative on the Agent tab.
  4. Look at the Diagram tab to see how your setup hangs together.
  5. If you have time: the later labs, or a practice exercise from Labs and practice.

The tabs: Lab (the lab you're on), Admin center (where you set things up), Diagram (your environment as a picture), Phone (a simulated phone to call in) and Agent (the representative's and supervisor's workspace). On the Phone, Agent and Diagram tabs a bar shows the contact center's time; operating hours and waiting times follow that clock.

Something odd? Files › Start again undoes everything you changed in the environment for the lab or exercise you're on.

Sending feedback

Select Feedback at the top, any time, as often as you like. Small things are welcome too: a confusing word, a button in the wrong place.

  1. What is it about? Something doesn't work · Something in the content is wrong or unclear · An idea or a wish · A question · Something else.
  2. Your feedback: what you did, what you expected, and what happened. Up to 2000 characters.
  3. Your name and email are optional. Leave your email if you'd like an answer.
  4. Include what I was doing in the training is on: it sends the lab and part, the tab, your class code and your browser and screen size. The form shows exactly what's sent; untick it if you prefer.
  5. Attach my environment (off unless you tick it) sends what you set up in the training, so we can see what you see. Very helpful when a check doesn't tick off.

You get a reference, such as 20261113132602-n6qpcw. Mention it if you contact us about it.

Send feedback from the screen where it happened, so the context that's sent is right.

Your data

  • In a class the training keeps your name, a random id, your personal code and your work on the class's labs and exercises. No account, no email.
  • On your own your work stays in your browser. Nothing is sent, except feedback you choose to send.
  • Feedback is kept with what you chose to include. Only the training's admins read it.
  • Nothing connects to a real Dynamics 365 environment, and all phone numbers are ones reserved for fiction. Don't enter real customer data or real phone numbers.

Good to know

  • It's a simulation. Menu and field names follow Microsoft's, but the screens are our own and only cover what the labs need. Not every button of the real product is there.
  • It's a test version. Things may change or break. If you get stuck, send feedback and try Files › Start again, or another lab.
  • Feedback limit: up to 20 a day from one browser.
  • Product facts come from Microsoft Learn, quoted with a link. If something seems different from what you know of the product, please tell us.

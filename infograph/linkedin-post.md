You can set up a trial of Dynamics 365 Contact Center and click around. But an empty environment doesn't teach you anything.

Where do you start? Why does a call end up in the wrong queue? Did you set up that routing rule right, or does it only look right?

That's why I built Promethean CCaaS Training: a hands-on training that tells you what to do and why, and checks what you built.

🧭 11 labs in order, from the first call to a full contact center
📖 Every lab: Learn, Do and Challenge
✅ Checks tick off as you build, so you know straight away whether you got it right
🛠️ Practice that tests understanding: fix a broken contact center, predict where a call goes
🎧 Call your own contact center and take the call as a representative
👩‍🏫 Trainers share one class code and see where people get stuck

It runs as a simulation in your browser. Not to avoid a tenant, but because that's what lets the training see your work, give hints and keep mistakes safe.
